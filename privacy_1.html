Privacy Policy

ticket-importer — last updated August 2026

Scope

ticket-importer is a private tool used solely by its author on the author's own accounts. It is not distributed and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the account owner's explicit authorization, the application reads messages and attachments from a personal Gmail account and writes files to a personal Google Drive folder.

Processing and storage

Processing runs on a private server controlled by the author. No data is shared with third parties, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model. Files remain in the account owner's Google Drive.

Tokens

Google authorization tokens are stored encrypted on that server and can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Use the contact address shown on the Google consent screen.
